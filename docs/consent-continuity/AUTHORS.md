## On how it was reviewed

Every unit was written by one author and attacked by another. That is not a courtesy pass: the
review immediately before publication returned a hold, not an approval, and the findings it raised
were ones the author's own automated checks structurally could not see.

That result is the argument for unit 03 (review symmetry) in miniature — human-written and
agent-written work reviewed to the same standard, by someone who did not write it.

The epistemic claim in unit 05 — that peer review catches what self-review cannot — is stated there
as a hypothesis with its sample size given, not as a finding. We are deliberately not overstating it.
